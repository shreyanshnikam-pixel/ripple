# Ripple demo: voiceover script

Read this over `ripple-demo.mp4` (2:55). The first minute is the problem, with real examples; the
rest is the demo. Speak at a normal, calm pace; the times are when each line starts. If you fall
behind, skip a sentence rather than rush.

This script covers everything the 2026 rules ask the video to include: your name, the app's
name, its purpose in one sentence, its target audience, the tools and languages used, and a demo
of what it does.

**Notes**

- The survey answers and the two students in the class are sample data added for the demo, and
  the captions say so ("demo answers", "demo students"). If you run a real survey at school,
  you can add: "When I asked ___ students at Livingston, ___ percent supported it, and Ripple
  predicted ___."
- The AI-help line at 2:28 matches the rules, which require AI use to be disclosed. Keep it, and
  give the full details in your written AI disclosure.
- Record in a quiet room, with the mic about a hand's width from your mouth. In CapCut, iMovie or
  Clipchamp, put the video on the timeline, record your voice on a second track, then nudge each
  line to start with its scene.
